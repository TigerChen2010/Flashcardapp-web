Privacy

For Flashcards and IB Tracker, apps made by Tiger Chen. Last updated 7 November 2026.

The short version

Your study data stays with you. The apps don't sell, share or analyse it, and there are no ads or trackers.

Where your data is kept

  • On your device. Sets, progress, plans and settings are saved on the computer or browser you use.
  • In your own Google Drive, if you sign in. Signing in with Google is optional. If you do, the apps save a copy in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive so your devices stay in sync. Only the app can see that folder; it can't see any of your other files.
  • Google Calendar (IB Tracker only, if you turn it on). IB Tracker can copy your timetable into your Google Calendar so you can see it on your phone.

What Google sign-in is used for

Your name and email are shown in Settings so you know which account is signed in. Access to Google Drive is limited to the app's own hidden folder. The apps never read, change or delete anything else in your Google account.

Reminders

If you turn on phone reminders in Flashcards, a small reminder service stores an anonymous device ID, your browser's notification address and the times and short texts of your upcoming reminders. It doesn't store your name, email or flashcards. Turning reminders off deletes this.

Removing your data

Sign out in Settings, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app or clearing the website's data removes the copy on your device.
